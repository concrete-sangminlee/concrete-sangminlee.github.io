I'm a Ph.D. candidate in the Interdisciplinary Program in Artificial Intelligence at Seoul National University, working in the Hi-performance Structural Engineering Lab with Prof. Thomas H.-K. Kang. My research asks how machine learning can make the inspection and design of buildings more reliable: automating impact-echo testing of concrete, monitoring structural condition from sensor data, estimating wind loads from fewer measurements, and grounding language models in building codes.

Before the Ph.D., I received an M.S. in structural engineering and a B.S. in architectural engineering, with a double major in electrical and computer engineering, both from SNU.
