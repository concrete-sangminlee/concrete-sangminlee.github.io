Presented our research on machine learning-assisted automated impact-echo testing for concrete structures. The study investigated the feasibility of ML models for automatic classification of impact-echo test results, achieving reliable detection of shallow delamination and other defect types.

**Key Points:**
- Comparison of ML approaches with conventional methods
- Universal classifier development using open-source databases
- Improved efficiency in impact-echo testing
- Practical applications in structural health monitoring

**Publication:** Journal of Nondestructive Evaluation (2025)
