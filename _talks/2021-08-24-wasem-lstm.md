Presented our research on predicting wind pressure coefficients on high-rise building facades using LSTM RNN models. The method enables sensor reduction while maintaining prediction accuracy.

**Key Points:**
- LSTM networks for time series prediction
- Wind pressure coefficient prediction
- Sensor reduction for cost efficiency
- Application to wind-resistant design
