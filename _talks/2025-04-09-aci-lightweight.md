Presented our research on lightweight machine learning models for concrete crack image classification. The study addresses the challenge of balancing accuracy with computational efficiency for on-site applications.

**Key Points:**
- Random Forest with HOG and LBP features
- Comparison with CNN models
- Edge device deployment
- Real-time structural health monitoring
