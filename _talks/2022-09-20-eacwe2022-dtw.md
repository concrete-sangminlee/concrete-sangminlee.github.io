Presented our research on clustering wind pressure taps using dynamic time warping. The method enables efficient sensor placement and wind pressure analysis for high-rise buildings.

**Key Points:**
- Dynamic time warping for time series analysis
- Wind pressure tap clustering
- Sensor reduction strategies
- Application to building design
