Hi, I'm Sang Min Lee. I am currently a Ph.D. candidate in the Interdisciplinary Program in Artificial Intelligence at Seoul National University. My research focuses on applying machine learning to concrete structures and materials, wind engineering, non-destructive testing, and structural health monitoring.

Research Interests
======
- Lightweight machine learning models for concrete crack image classification
- Multi-class deep SVDD for concrete building monitoring
- Large language model framework specialized for structural design codes
- Neural network-based wind speed prediction using satellite images
- Automated impact-echo testing using machine learning
- Machine learning models for concrete compressive strength prediction
- LSTM RNN-based wind pressure coefficient prediction for high-rise building facades

Education
